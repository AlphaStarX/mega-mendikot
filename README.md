# ♠ Mega Mindikot 5v5 ♥

**Mega Mindikot 5v5** is a fast-paced, team-based **trick-taking card game** for
**10 players** (5 vs 5), inspired by the traditional Indian card game *Mendikot*.

Two teams of five race to capture valuable cards. The twist: instead of a normal
deck, the game uses a giant **192-card mega deck** made from six combined decks —
so identical cards appear six times. That creates ties, surprises, and a signature
rule that makes the game unique.

A typical match lasts **15–20 minutes**.

> 📖 **For the complete and final word on every rule, see the
> [Official Player Rulebook](./RULEBOOK.md).** This README is a quick primer.

---

## The Objective

> **Be the first team to capture 13 of the 24 Tens in the deck.**

**Tens** (the "Mendis") are the only cards that matter for winning. There are 24
of them in the mega deck. The moment a team's Ten-count hits **13**, the match
ends **immediately** and that team wins.

## Quick Start

1. **Open the game** and enter your name.
2. **Pick a mode:**
   - **Quick Match** — you're seated immediately and the empty seats fill with
     bots after a short wait, so you can play instantly.
   - **Create Private Room** — get a 4-letter room code and a shareable link to
     invite friends.
   - **Join** a friend's room by entering their 4-letter code.
3. **You're seated on Team A or Team B.** Play moves clockwise around the table.
4. **On your turn, click a playable card.** Cards with a **gold border** are
   legal; **dimmed** cards violate the follow-suit rule and can't be played.
5. Press the **`?`** button in the top-right during a match to open the full
   searchable Rules reference and the interactive "How to Play" tutorial.

> 💡 **Solo play:** Quick Match with 1 human + 9 bots is fully playable — the
> bots follow the complete strategy tree, including trump declaration.

## How a Match Works

### The Table (5 vs 5)
Ten seats (0–9) alternate teams around an oval table — **even seats = Team A,
odd seats = Team B** — so every player sits between two opponents.

### The Mega Deck & The Kitty
- **192 cards** total: six standard decks, modified to keep only ranks **7–A**
  (2s–6s removed). That gives **6 copies of every card** — including **6 copies
  of the 10 in each suit** = **24 Tens** in the deck.
- Each player is dealt **18 cards**; **12 cards** are set aside face-down as the
  **kitty**, in the middle of the table.

### Lead Selection
Before the first trick, there's a **lead-selection ceremony**: a separate
selection deck is dealt, one card per player, and the **highest card leads trick
1**. Ties trigger a shootout redraw until there's a single winner.

---

## How to Play a Trick

1. **The Lead.** The lead player plays the first card, which sets the **Lead
   Suit** for that trick.
2. **Follow Suit.** Every subsequent player **must play the lead suit** if they
   hold it.
3. **Play Off-Suit (and set Trump).** If you **can't** follow suit, you play a
   card of any other suit:
   - The **first off-suit card of the entire match** establishes the **Trump
     Suit**, permanently. Once set, trump is fixed for the whole match.
   - The **lead player can never declare trump** (they always follow their own
     lead suit).
4. **Trump beats everything.** Once trump exists, any trump card beats any
   lead-suit card. **Discards** (cards that are neither lead suit nor trump) can
   **never win** a trick.

---

## Winning a Trick

Once all 10 players have played, the trick resolves:

- **Highest trump** (if any trump was played) wins, **otherwise**
- **Highest lead-suit card** wins. Discards are ineligible.

### The Duplicate Rule ("Last Wins")
Because every card appears 6 times, ties are common. When identical cards tie
for the win, **the last one played wins**. Acting later in the turn order is an
advantage.

### The Kitty
Over the **first 12 tricks**, one hidden kitty card is revealed per trick and
**captured by the trick winner**. A kitty **Ten** counts toward your team's
score and can even trigger an instant win.

---

## Winning the Match

| Outcome | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Instant win** | First team to capture **13 Tens** wins immediately (checked after every trick). |
| **12-12 deadlock** | If both teams reach 12 Tens after all 18 tricks, the team that won the **most tricks** takes it. |
| **Draw** | If it's 12-12 *and* tricks are tied 9-9, the match is a draw. |

## Turn Timer & AFK

- You have **20 seconds** per play. A ring around the active player's avatar
  counts down; it flashes red at 5 seconds.
- **Time out twice in a row** and you're marked **AFK** — your plays are
  **auto-played instantly** until you play a card manually. Auto-play picks the
  safest legal card.

---

## Card Strength

```
7 < 8 < 9 < 10 < J < Q < K < A      (Ace is strongest, 7 is weakest)
```

- **The 10 is the only card that scores.** It's the "Mendi." There are 24 of
  them in the deck, and your team needs 13 to win.
- All other ranks only matter for **winning tricks** (which lets you capture
  the Tens inside them).
